Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Google Play purchases

Current Support Actua purchases are processed by Google Play. Request a refund through Google Play’s refund process, or email actua@azimulkabir.com for help. Include your order ID and a short description of the problem. Do not send card details or budget data.

Refund eligibility follows Google Play’s policies and applicable law. Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund an earlier payment. See our Terms of Service for purchase and subscription details.

Website purchases

Website checkout is not open yet. When it opens, Supporter Extras will be a one-time purchase sold by Paddle, our seller of record, under Paddle’s buyer terms.

  • 14 days. You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase. You do not need to give a reason.
  • How to ask. Email actua@azimulkabir.com with the email address you bought with, or the transaction ID from your Paddle receipt. You can also contact Paddle through the link in your receipt.
  • How you are paid back. Paddle refunds the original payment method. Your bank or card issuer decides how long it then takes to show.
  • What happens to the extras. After a refund, Actua will not renew access, and extras already checked on your device can keep working for up to 24 hours. Budgeting is never affected.
  • After 14 days. Email us and we will try to help. Your statutory rights do not change.

Google Play purchases are not affected: those refunds are handled as described above.

Nothing on this page limits your statutory consumer rights.
