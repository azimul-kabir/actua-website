Support Actua

Make Actua your own. Supporter Extras add personal touches to your Android budgeting app, and your purchase helps fund its development. Budgeting, sync, reports and backups stay free.

Supporter Extras

  • App icons: choose Dark, Mono or Light.
  • Accent colors: Teal, Amber, Rose, Forest, Ocean and Mono.
  • Catppuccin themes: Latte, Frappé, Macchiato and Mocha, with 14 accents.
  • Widget styles: Translucent, Solid dark, Solid light and Accent-tinted.
  • Celebrations: optional confetti when Ready to budget reaches zero or you finish a reconciliation.
  • A friendly greeting: useful counts, such as bills due this week, without showing amounts.

Current Google Play purchases are restored through the Google account that bought them. The standard direct-download APK does not currently offer Supporter Extras.
