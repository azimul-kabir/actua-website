The Google Play version offers optional Support Actua purchases:

Subscriptions

The monthly subscription renews automatically every month at the price shown when you subscribed, until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals, and your Supporter extras stay available until the end of the period you've already paid for. If we ever change the subscription price, Google Play will tell you before the new price applies.
